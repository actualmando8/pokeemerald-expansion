# Kanto ↔ Hoenn Two-Region Game Project

## FULL GAME PROGRESSION

---

### KANTO — ACT 1
- Start in Pallet Town, early routes, gyms proceed normally
- Brock, Misty, Surge — standard Kanto early game
- In Vermilion you meet a man who fled Hoenn. He mentions trouble back home and gives you the Hoenn Ticket
- Kanto continues — Cerulean, Lavender, Celadon, Koga, Sabrina, Blaine, Giovanni

---

### HOENN — FIRST AVAILABLE AFTER VERMILION
- Ferry from Vermilion directly to Slateport
- Arrive during Team Aqua's museum attack, pulled into their conflict
- Mauville — 1st Hoenn badge
- Lavaridge — 2nd Hoenn badge
- Briney available after 2 badges — gives an excuse until then
- Briney takes you to Dewford
- Dewford — 3rd Hoenn badge
- From Dewford sail back to Slateport or to southern Route 104
- Southern Route 104 leads directly to Petalburg Town
- Optional detour — head south from Petalburg through Route 102 to Oldale and Route 101 to Littleroot for Hoenn starter
- Littleroot naturally gated by Briney access — no additional block needed
- After obtaining Hoenn starter, Hoenn rival encountered north of Oldale on Route 103
- Rival battles will follow vanilla Emerald progression from this point
- Head north through Petalburg Woods and northern Route 104 to Rustboro
- Rustboro — 4th Hoenn badge
- Head back south to Petalburg
- Petalburg — 5th Hoenn badge
- NPC on Route 118 blocks passage east toward Fortree until badge gate met
- Ferry NPC comments that a trainer with your badge count should keep pushing before heading home
- Head back to Slateport and ferry back to Vermilion to continue Kanto

---

### KANTO — CONTINUING
- Player returns to Kanto and continues badge progression
- Cerulean, Lavender, Celadon, Koga, Sabrina, Blaine, Giovanni
- Rocket activity grows more visible and serious

---

### MID GAME — BOUNCING BETWEEN REGIONS
- Player freely travels Vermilion ↔ Slateport building badges in both regions
- Kanto and Hoenn stories escalate in parallel
- Rocket activity grows in Kanto
- Aqua and Magma conflict intensifies in Hoenn
- Weather becomes subtly unstable in both regions
- Player can continue Kanto at any point but the intended flow keeps both regions progressing together
- Scientists in Mauville studying a meteor — early thread planted, details to be developed

---

### SEVII — FIRST VISIT (after defeating Blaine, same as vanilla)
- Tri-Pass unlocks One through Three Islands from Vermilion
- Original FRLG story and wild Pokémon encounters preserved
- Noland, Greta, Tucker as island gatekeepers (reusing Frontier Brain characters with new teams)
- Rocket hints planted: shipments from the south, a grunt spotted near Bond Bridge
- NPCs on One Island mention strangers arriving by boat at night
- Return to main game with more questions than answers

---

### SEVII — SECOND VISIT (7th badge in either region)
- Four and Five Islands unlock
- Spenser and Anabel as gatekeepers
- Icefall Cave: Lorelei confronts Rockets, hints someone is reorganizing them
- Rocket scientist on Five Island Meadow drops document: "Project Rebirth — Johto Division" and "the boss's son refuses to cooperate" (first Silver breadcrumb)
- Can be accessed before or after Rayquaza event

---

### HOENN — ACT 3 (after 5 Hoenn and 5 Kanto badges)
- NPC on Route 118 steps aside
- Fortree unlocks
- Story escalates through Mossdeep and Sootopolis
- Kyogre and Groudon awaken and begin battling
- Ferry suspended for the duration of the battle — brief dramatic window
- Kanto too far to be seriously affected — minor unsettling weather, a few NPC references
- Johto closer to Hoenn bears the brunt — Johto residents in Sevii reference it if visited afterward
- Man in Vermilion watches the skies anxiously
- Rayquaza descends, Kyogre and Groudon retreat
- Crisis over, ferry immediately resumes

---

### LATE GAME — FINISHING BOTH REGIONS
- Return to Kanto and Hoenn to finish remaining badges
- Rocket subplot concludes as vanilla with stronger remnants in Sevii
- Stronger Giovanni fight in Sevii Islands as true final Rocket confrontation
- Aqua and Magma remnants dealt with in Hoenn
- Kanto Elite Four beaten
- Hoenn Elite Four beaten
- Hoenn rival if obtained appears in Celadon City after 8 badges in both regions — casual encounter, full power battle, payoff for players who did the Littleroot detour

---

### BATTLE FRONTIER UNLOCKS

- Player begins working toward remaining conditions

- Requires BOTH: FLAG_IS_KANTO_CHAMPION AND FLAG_JOHTO_ACCESS_UNLOCKED (Sevii Giovanni defeated)

- When both conditions met, guard steps aside, passage south to Route 26 opens
- Full Johto region: 8 gym badges, Giovanni/Silver storyline, 20+ routes, dungeons
- HGSS + Crystal wild Pokémon encounters (96 encounter areas, Lv40-70, scaled for post-Champion)
- **Ho-Oh** — Tin Tower Roof (Lv65 legendary battle)
- **Lugia** — Whirl Islands Lugia Chamber (Lv65 legendary battle)
- Silver appears as recurring rival throughout Johto (Giovanni's son, rejecting his father's path)
- Johto story climax: Giovanni confrontation (Radio Tower or Team Rocket HQ)
- **Celebi** — Ilex Forest Shrine (Lv55, gated behind all 8 Johto badges)
- Navel Rock DISABLED (Four Island elder gives Johto lore instead)

### BATTLE DOME — TRUE FINAL CHALLENGE

Unlocks only after all of the following are completed:
- Kanto Elite Four beaten
- Hoenn Elite Four beaten
- All 8 Johto gym badges (FLAG_ALL_JOHTO_BADGES)
- Birth Island — Deoxys resolved
- Faraway Island — Mew obtained
- Jirachi resolved
- Cerulean Cave — Mewtwo

When every condition is met the Dome doors open. No fanfare. Just open.
- Lorelei (Ice, Lv72)
- Will (Psychic, Lv72 — Johto E4 member, replaced Bruno)
- Phoebe (Ghost, Lv72)
- Lance (Dragon, Lv75)
- Champion: Steven (Lv78)
